# Photo prompts (ChatGPT Images or any image AI)

These are **preview photos** so the site looks right while we wait for Cuplis's real ones.
Before the site goes public, swap them for his own cuts: the site tells clients "real cuts",
and showing AI haircuts as his work would mislead them. His Instagram photos are a good
source (with his and the client's OK).

## How to use

1. Start every prompt with the **style block** below, then add the shot prompt.
2. Ask for **portrait 4:5** (1080 × 1350). Save as `.jpg`.
3. Save each file with the exact name shown, into this folder:
   `public/images/` (create it). Cuts go in `public/images/cuts/`.
4. Tell me when they're in and I'll switch the site over.

Tip: generate a cut's two angles in the **same chat** ("same man, same haircut, now from the side")
so the person and the haircut stay consistent.

## Style block (paste first, every time)

> Photorealistic editorial photo, shot on 35mm film, like a still from a moody indie film.
> Indonesian barbershop at night, warm tungsten light, dark charcoal wall background,
> shallow depth of field, soft film grain, slightly muted colors, natural skin texture.
> No text, no logos, no watermark. Portrait orientation 4:5.
